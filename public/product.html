Product Information

Tide Simply Free & Gentle Liquid Detergent cleans thoroughly while being especially formulated for sensitive skin without any synthetic scents or dyes.

  • check_small Gentle on sensitive skin and hypoallergenic formulation.
  • check_small Cleans fabrics down to the fiber to lift soil and residue.
  • check_small Compatible with both standard and High-Efficiency (HE) washing machines in cold or warm cycles.

How to Measure:

Use dosing cap to measure. Fill to Bar 1 for medium loads, Bar 3 for full large loads. Pour into detergent dispenser tray or directly into wash drum before adding clothes.

Active Cleaning Agents:

Biodegradable surfactants (anionic and nonionic), enzyme stabilizing aids, and water softeners. Contains 0% phosphate.
